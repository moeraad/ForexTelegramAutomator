Billing & Subscriptions — money model

Two paying relationships pointing opposite ways: subscribers pay the platform; the platform pays providers. COGS (AI + MetaApi) is variable, so it's funded by the platform fee we control.

2 · What a subscriber pays (hybrid)

Component A · platform-priced

Platform access fee

Funds the costs we bear. We control this number, so margin is protected.

  • Execution tier (self-hosted EA vs MetaApi managed)
  • Monthly AI interpreter quota
  • Dashboard, infra, support
Covers our COGS → kept by platform
Component B · provider-priced

Per-channel subscription

Set by each provider. One per channel the subscriber copies. Marketplace model.

  • Provider sets their own monthly price
  • Subscriber copying 3 channels pays 3 channel prices
  • Platform takes a flat commission (~25%)
Split: ~75% provider · ~25% platform

4 · Per-channel AI cost — metered & variable (quality never degraded)

AI inference runs once per channel message, shared by all that channel's subscribers. Cost is not capped — the full-quality interpreter runs every time. Instead it's metered as a variable cost and passed through to the provider (deducted from their payout). The cheap triage gate filters noise first, so metered cost tracks real signal volume, not chatter.

5 · Plumbing

Processor

Stripe + Stripe Connect

  • Subscriptions, proration, Stripe Tax, dunning, customer portal
  • Connect (Express) for provider payouts: KYC/AML + automated revenue-share transfers
  • We never hold funds → avoid money-transmitter licensing
State & safety

Source of truth & lifecycle

  • Stripe = billing truth, mirrored to Postgres via webhooks for fast access checks
  • Cancellation undeploys MetaApi account immediately → stops hourly meter
  • Failed payment → degrade gracefully (manage-to-close, never abandon an open trade)
